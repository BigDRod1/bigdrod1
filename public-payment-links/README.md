## Confidence levels

- **high** — listed on the person's own tip/donate page
- **medium** — listed on a link-in-bio they appear to control
- **platform** — listed on Furipay / Tipfall / Tiplor (often Stripe/card/crypto, not Cash App)

## Important

Viral "celebrity Cash App giveaway" lists are scams. Cash App and Venmo do **not** publish a public celebrity directory. Always verify handles on official channels before sending money.
